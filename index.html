The process

Five layers of
skilled craftsmanship

Each piece takes 12 hours of skilled handwork — a deliberate, unhurried process that cannot be rushed or replicated by machine.

1

Surface preparation

Substrate cleaned, primed and sealed to receive the plaster perfectly.

2

Base plaster coat

Italian lime-based decorative plaster applied and hand-burnished to create a pearl-like lustre background across the panel and frame.

3

Crest modelling

The design is built up directly in the surface using a second application of lime-based plaster, shaped and refined entirely by hand.

4

Metal leaf application

Blue-coloured metal leaf is laid over the crest with sizing adhesive — giving a luminous, light-shifting metallic finish.

5

Protective sealing

A transparent water-based glossy coating seals the full surface, protecting the artwork for decades — even in high-traffic environments.

Materials

Four premium materials,
one exceptional result

🏺

Italian lime-based decorative plaster

Traditional Marmorino/Venetian formula applied and burnished by hand. Creates extraordinary surface depth and a natural pearl-like lustre — the foundation of every piece.

Base & crest layer
✨

Blue coloured metal leaf

Hand-laid metallic leaf applied over the modelled crest with sizing adhesive. Catches and shifts with the light — no two viewing angles look identical.

Decorative finish
💧

Water-based glossy protective coating

Transparent, low-VOC sealant that locks in the finish and protects against wear, UV, and moisture. Suitable for public and high-traffic spaces.

Top coat sealant

Venetian-plastered timber frame

A raw timber frame of your chosen species, fully covered in the same Italian lime-based decorative plaster — creating a seamless, unified luxury object rather than a picture in a frame.
