The process

Five layers of
skilled craftsmanship

Each piece takes 12 hours of skilled handwork — a deliberate, unhurried process that cannot be rushed or replicated by machine.

1

Surface preparation

Substrate cleaned, primed and sealed to receive the plaster perfectly.

2

Base plaster coat

Italian lime-based decorative plaster applied and hand-burnished to create a pearl-like lustre background across the panel and frame.

3

Crest modelling

The design is built up directly in the surface using a second application of lime-based plaster, shaped and refined entirely by hand.

4

Metal leaf application

Blue-coloured metal leaf is laid over the crest with sizing adhesive — giving a luminous, light-shifting metallic finish.

5

Protective sealing

A transparent water-based glossy coating seals the full surface, protecting the artwork for decades — even in high-traffic environments.

Application process

1
Surface preparation — substrate cleaned, primed and sealed ready to receive plaster.
2
Base plaster coat — Italian lime-based decorative plaster applied and hand-burnished to create the full-surface pearl-like lustre background.
3
Crest modelling — crest design built up directly into the surface using a second application of Italian lime-based decorative plaster, hand-worked to form the relief.
4
Metal leaf application — blue coloured metal leaf is laid over the crest, giving a luminous, light-shifting metallic finish.
5
Protective sealing — transparent water-based glossy coating applied across the full surface, protecting the artwork for long-term durability.
This process can be applied directly onto walls or onto framed panels in any size. All commissions are entirely bespoke — framing, sizing, and finish are tailored to each client's requirements.
